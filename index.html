WELCOME TO OUR PLAYHOUSE

Little steps today. Bright discoveries tomorrow.

At Little Star Playhouse, we believe every child is unique and deserves an affectionate, pressure-free environment to flourish. Located in the heart of Janakpurdham, our playhouse blends early Montessori and ECCD principles with warm parental care.

  • Holistic Early Growth: Focusing on language, motor coordination, habits, and emotional well-being.
  • Joyful Activities Every Day: Singing rhymes, creative drawing, interactive storytelling, and dancing.
  • Open Communication with Parents: Regular photo updates and friendly progress conversations via WhatsApp.

SAFETY, HYGIENE & TRUST

Why Janakpur Parents Trust Little Star

We know placing your child in someone else's care takes trust. Here is how we ensure their safety, joy, and healthy development every single day.

🛡️

Secure & Child-Safe Campus

Controlled gate entry, round-the-clock supervision, cushioned flooring, rounded furniture, and continuous monitoring.

🧼

High Hygiene Standards

Daily sanitised toys, child-sized clean washrooms, filtered pure drinking water, and trained hand-washing habits.

👩‍🏫

Trained & Affectionate Staff

Patient educators with Montessori/ECCD orientation who treat every little child with warmth, love, and respect.

🛏️

Quiet Nap & Resting Area

Dedicated, sanitised beds and peaceful environment for children needing an afternoon nap during daycare hours.

📱

Active Parent Communication

Direct WhatsApp access, daily activity updates, dietary check-ins, and scheduled parent meetings.

🩺

First Aid & Health Preparedness

Well-equipped first aid kit, strict protocols for minor illness, and rapid coordination with local health clinics.
